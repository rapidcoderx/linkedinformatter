Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make bold text on LinkedIn?

LinkedIn doesn't support native bold in posts. Paste your text into the editor, select it, and click the 𝗕 button (or press Ctrl+B). Copy the result and paste directly into LinkedIn — it renders as bold on all devices.

What Unicode text styles are available?

20+ styles: 𝗕𝗼𝗹𝗱, 𝘐𝘵𝘢𝘭𝘪𝘤, 𝑩𝒐𝒍𝒅 𝑰𝒕𝒂𝒍𝒊𝒄, 𝙼𝚘𝚗𝚘𝚜𝚙𝚊𝚌𝚎, 𝓢𝓬𝓻𝓲𝓹𝓽, 𝔉𝔯𝔞𝔨𝔱𝔲𝔯, 𝔻𝕠𝕦𝕓𝕝𝕖-𝕊𝕥𝕣𝕦𝕔𝕜, Ⓒⓘⓡⓒⓛⓔⓓ, and more. All display correctly in LinkedIn posts, comments, and messages.

Can I convert Markdown to LinkedIn format?

Yes. Click Import MD or press Ctrl+M, paste your Markdown, and the tool converts headings, bold, italic, code blocks, lists, and blockquotes into LinkedIn-compatible Unicode formatting.

Is this tool free? Do I need an account?

Completely free — no sign-up, no account, no watermarks. All formatting happens in your browser. Your draft is auto-saved to localStorage so you never lose your work.
